## Suggested post

Do you know how much your subscriptions add up to each year?

We built Subscription Cost Analyzer, a free tool to help you see recurring spending in one place and explore potential savings.

• Add monthly, quarterly, and annual subscriptions.
• See monthly averages and yearly spending.
• Mark subscriptions you're considering cancelling and compare the potential savings.
• Choose one currency for your list. No sign-up required.

Your own entries are saved in supported browsers when local storage and safe cross-tab coordination are available, not uploaded to our server. You can delete this app's saved data whenever you want.

Try it here: https://rdanusha.github.io/subscription-cost-analyzer/

Open-source code: https://github.com/rdanusha/subscription-cost-analyzer

Built by rdanusha.

This is a planning tool, not a cancellation service. It does not cancel subscriptions, and potential savings depend on your providers' terms. No currency conversion is performed.

Try it and tell us what would make it more useful.
